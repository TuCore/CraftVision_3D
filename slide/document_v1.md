# **CRAFTVISION 3D — MVP RESULTS REPORT**

**October 2026**

---

## **PART I. BUSINESS OVERVIEW**

### **Slide 1\. Project Overview**

**CraftVision 3D** is a gifting and creative platform that combines handmade products, AI-assisted gift and greeting ideation, personalized 3D greeting cards, and smart NFC technology to make gift-giving more personal, interactive, and memorable.

* **Products:** Finished and customizable handmade gifts (custom iron-on charms, animal keychains, crochet items, pipe-cleaner flowers, mini building blocks, plushies, lucky stars), AI crafting and gifting assistant, and personalized 3D digital greeting cards.  
* **Core Value:** Combining tangible handmade gifts with personal messages, beginner-friendly creative support, and interactive digital experiences.  
* **Business Objective:** To understand customer needs, evaluate market interest across online and direct sales channels, and explore the commercial potential of the platform.

---

### **Slide 2\. Market Segmentation, Target Customers (ICP) & Core Value Proposition**

#### **2.1. Market Segmentation**

* **Demographics:**  
  * **Age Groups:**  
    * Under 18: High school students.  
    * **From 18 to 22: University and college students (Gen Z in education — core segment).**  
    * From 23 to 27: Young working adults (Gen Z in the workforce).  
    * Above 27: Mature adults.  
  * **Occupation & Location:** The majority of respondents and early buyers are University/College Students, initially focused on university areas in Thu Duc City, Ho Chi Minh City.  
  * **Gender:** Fairly balanced between male and female customers, indicating that handmade crafting and gifting are no longer exclusive to women.  
      
* **Behavioral Characteristics:**  
  * **Interest Levels:** Moderate to very high interest in handmade products across the surveyed audience.  
  * **Crafting Frequency:** Primarily occasional crafters (making or buying items every few months or for special occasions) and those who rarely craft on their own.  
  * **Usage Occasions:** Mainly purchased or created as gifts for family and friends, for relaxation and entertainment, or for personal everyday decoration.  
  * **Information Searching:** High reliance on short-form video platforms such as TikTok and YouTube for crafting tutorials and gift inspiration.  
  * **Product Preferences:** Strong preference for bracelets, greeting cards, knitted and crocheted items, custom keychains/charms, and scented candles.  
* **Psychographics:**  
  * **Emotional Value & Connection:** The strongest motivation is *"Making/giving gifts for relatives and friends"*—handmade items are valued as a heartfelt medium to express affection.  
  * **Stress Relief Needs ("Healing"):** *"Relaxation and entertainment"* accounts for a major share of user motivation, serving as a way to unwind from academic and work pressures.  
  * **Self-Expression:** The desire to *"Make or own personal decorations for daily use"* reflects an appreciation for uniqueness and personal identity.

#### **2.2. Targeting Strategy**

**Strategic Focus:** Targeting professional or highly experienced crafters would be ineffective. CraftVision 3D focuses on customer segments with the highest emotional intent and the most aligned pain points.

* **Primary Target Audience:**  
  * **Profile:** **University/College Students (Ages 18–22)** who want to make or customize handmade items primarily for special occasions or casual stress relief.  
  * **Why Target Them:** They represent the vast majority of the market data and are highly enthusiastic (*"Interested"* to *"Very interested"*) about an AI-assisted platform that simplifies the process—directly addressing their lack of ideas, limited crafting skills, and difficulty finding beginner-friendly materials and tutorials.  
  * **Willingness to Pay:** Operating on a strict student budget, they expect highly accessible pricing—primarily **under VND 30,000** or **VND 30,000 – 50,000** for premium AI features or digital cards, and **VND 15,000 – 30,000** for small handmade items.  
* **Secondary Target Audience:**  
  * **Profile:** **Young Adults Intrigued by Tech-Integrated Physical Gifts (NFC/QR)** and personalized keepsakes (alongside early group and corporate gift buyers).  
  * **Why Target Them:** They show strong interest in interactive features such as unlocking secret messages, 3D scenes, and memorial photos or videos via smartphone tap.  
  * **Willingness to Pay:** Willing to pay a higher price for finished, customized, or tech-integrated physical products—typically **VND 30,000 – 50,000** or **VND 50,000 – 100,000** per item.  
* **Gift Recipients (End-Users of the Experience):**  
  * Friends, couples, teachers, and loved ones who receive CraftVision 3D gifts and tap the NFC tag or scan a code to open a personal message and 3D greeting card directly on their mobile browser without installing any app.

#### **2.3. Key Customer Pain Points**

* **Lack of ideas** when planning or designing a meaningful gift.  
* **Difficulty finding suitable tutorials** and **overly complicated instructions**.  
* **Limited crafting skills** and **time constraints** when trying to make handmade gifts from scratch.  
* Traditional gifts can be expensive or feel too generic, while paper cards are easy to lose and offer limited interactive experiences.

#### **2.4. Core Value Proposition & Unique Selling Point (USP): Phygital Gifting**

**Physical Handmade Gifts \+ Digital Memories \= A More Personal Gifting Experience**

* **Handmade & Custom Gifts:** Small, creative physical gifts starting at **VND 15,000**, eliminating the need to craft everything from scratch.  
* **AI-Assisted Personalization:** Helps users overcome a lack of ideas with guided message creation, custom charm designs, and customizable 3D greeting cards.  
* **Interactive NFC Experience:** Connects a physical gift to its digital 3D card, photos, and personal message with a single smartphone tap.

### **Slide 4\. User Experience (UX/UI) and Customer Journey**

#### **Customer Journey**

**Discover Ideas & Browse Gifts → Personalize Product & Greeting Card → Place an Order & Pay → Tap or Scan the NFC Tag → View the Digital Gift**

#### **Key UX/UI Features**

* Browse the product catalog, explore gifting ideas, and compare available handmade gifts.  
* Choose a 3D greeting card template and add a personal message (with optional AI writing assistance).

#### **Prototype Evaluation**

* The MVP brings together all core steps of the gifting process, from discovering gift ideas and customizing products to opening digital content via NFC.  
* Further usability testing and customer feedback from both gift buyers and recipients will help simplify the purchasing flow and enhance the digital unboxing experience.

### **Slide 7\. Smart NFC Usage and Customer Engagement**

#### **Customer Engagement by Smart Gift & Gifting Occasion**

| Smart Gift & 3D Card Pairing | Gifting Occasion | Assigned Gifts | Total NFC Taps (Scans) | Avg. Taps / Gift | Customer Behavior Insight |
| :---- | :---- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---- |
| **Custom Iron-On Charm \+ *Confession Heart Key* 3D Card** | Couple / Confession | 2 | **19** | **9.5** | Highest repeat opens; recipients replay the 3D scene & show friends |
| **Crochet & Plush Keychain \+ *Valentine Rose* 3D Card** | Birthday / Romantic | 2 | **11** | **5.5** | Strong emotional retention; recipients revisit personal photos & music |

| **Total / Average** | **All Occasions** | **6** | **35** | **5.83** | **100% of NFC-enabled gifts were opened multiple times** |

#### **What These Results Tell Us**

* **High Repeat Engagement (5.83 Taps per Gift):** Unlike traditional paper cards that are read once and put away, recipients of NFC-enabled gifts tap their keychain or gift an average of nearly **6 times** to replay the 3D animation, music, and personal message.  
* **Strongest Traction in Romantic & Personalized Gifts:** Gifts paired with romantic 3D themes (*Confession Heart Key* and *Valentine Rose*) generated **85.7% of all NFC interactions (30 out of 35 taps)**, reaching up to **9.5 taps per gift** as recipients shared the experience with friends.  
* **Zero-Friction Unboxing:** Because the 3D card opens instantly in the smartphone browser with a single tap, 100% of assigned smart gifts were successfully unlocked by recipients without requiring any app download.

## **PART IV. CONCLUSION AND NEXT STEPS**

### **Slide 9\. Conclusion and Next Steps**

#### **Key Results**

* **Validated Target Audience & Pricing:** Confirmed strong product-market fit with **university students (18–22)** and **tech-curious young adults**, with order values clustering around the target **VND 15,000 – 60,000** range.  
* **Platform & Catalog Readiness:** **31 registered accounts** (71% via Google), **16 active products**, and **54 available 3D greeting card templates**.  
* **Strong Multi-Channel Sales:** **33 orders recorded** (61 units sold) generating **VND 1,595,000 in merchandise GMV**, with **21 orders (VND 1,150,000) already paid**.  
* **High Demand for Custom & Corporate Gifts:** **Custom Iron-On Keychains** led sales at **VND 565,000 (35.4% of GMV)**, complemented by the first **corporate bulk order worth VND 300,000**.  
* **Digital Experience Validation:** **7 personalized 3D cards** created, **35 NFC scans** recorded across 6 assigned tags, and **1 successful standalone 3D card payment worth VND 49,000**.

#### **Current Limitations**

* **Decentralized Order Records:** 25 out of 33 orders from Facebook and direct sales representatives were tracked manually on spreadsheets rather than centralized on the online platform.  
* **Pending Fulfillment & Uncollected Receivables:** **4 pre-paid orders (VND 285,000)** are awaiting delivery, and **12 orders (VND 445,000)** remain undelivered and unpaid.  
* **Unconfirmed Profitability:** Product material costs, crafting labor, NFC tag costs, and operating expenses have not yet been deducted to establish net margins.

#### **Next Steps**

1. **Unify Multi-Channel Order Management:** Centralize all Website, Facebook, and Direct Sales orders into a single tracking system so platform metrics reflect 100% of actual sales.  
2. **Fulfill Pending Orders & Collect Receivables:** Complete delivery for the 4 pre-paid orders (VND 285,000) and fulfill the 12 pending orders (VND 445,000) to collect remaining payments.  
3. **Scale Short-Form Video Marketing (TikTok & YouTube):** Produce short-form videos showcasing custom charm crafting and NFC tap-to-reveal reactions to capture the target student audience where they search for ideas.  
4. **Conduct Customer & Recipient Research:** Collect feedback from the first 33 buyers and their gift recipients to refine beginner-friendly customization and the NFC gifting experience.  
5. **Evaluate Unit Economics & Profitability:** Calculate exact material costs, crafting time, NFC costs, and net profit margins per product line.
